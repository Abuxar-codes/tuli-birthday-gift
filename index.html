A letter for you

I wanted you to have this.

Happy birthday, my love. ❤️

I’ve been thinking about what I should write to you, and honestly, I don’t think I can put two years, eight months, and thirteen days of us into a few paragraphs. There’s just too much between us. So many memories, conversations, laughs, arguments, good days, bad days, and moments that only you and I would understand.

Two years, eight months, and thirteen days is a lot. And when I look back at everything we’ve been through, I realize that our relationship hasn’t always been easy. We’ve had times when we were so close, and we’ve also had times when it felt like we were slowly becoming strangers. We’ve fought, misunderstood each other, hurt each other, and there were moments when things between us felt really weak.

I’m not going to pretend those things never happened, because they did. But I also don’t think those moments define us.

What matters to me is that despite everything, somehow we kept finding our way back to each other. Even when things got difficult, there was still a part of me that didn’t want to let you go. Maybe that’s what all this time really means to me—not that everything was perfect, but that even when things weren’t perfect, you were still someone I wanted in my life.

Being in a long-distance relationship makes some things harder than they should be. There are so many moments when I wish I could just be there. I wish I could see you on your birthday, give you something with my own hands, watch your reaction, take you somewhere, or simply sit beside you without a screen between us.

But even with all these miles between us, you’ve still managed to become one of the closest people to my heart.

And I know I don’t always say everything I feel. Sometimes I probably don’t show you enough how much you mean to me. But I hope you know that you’ve left a place in my life that isn’t easy to replace. You’ve been a part of so many versions of me during this time, and I’ve watched you grow and change too. In a strange way, we’ve grown up alongside each other.

I don’t know exactly what the future is going to look like. I can’t promise that everything will always be easy, or that we’ll never fight again. But I can say that what we’ve had is real to me. And after everything we’ve been through, I still care about you deeply. I still want to see you happy. I still want to be someone you can count on. And I still want to make more memories with you.

Today isn't just about celebrating your birthday. It's also a reminder of how much life has happened between the two of us since we first became “us.”

So, happy birthday to my Tuli — my Tulip — the girl who has been a part of my life for two years, eight months, and thirteen days. ❤️

I hope this new year of your life gives you peace, happiness, success, and all the little things you've been wishing for. I hope you keep smiling, keep becoming the person you want to be, and never forget that somewhere, even from far away, there’s someone who genuinely cares about you and is always wishing the best for you.

And maybe one day, we won’t have to celebrate important days through a screen anymore.

Until then, I’ll keep holding onto what we have.

After all this time, after everything, I’m still here.

And I still choose you.

Happy birthday, my love. ❤️
